## 1. Contact

Call us at **+30 211-800-1320** or email **info@datamind.gr**.

## 2. Shipping

Send your storage device with our partner courier, at our expense.

## 3. Diagnosis

We diagnose the problem within 1–2 days, estimate the cost and time of the recovery, and let you know.

## 4. Recovery

Once you approve, we recover the data, make backup copies, send you a list of the recovered files and agree with you how they will be delivered.

## 5. Delivery

Your data is returned on a storage device (hard disk or USB flash disk), depending on its size.

![The data recovery process](/images/wp/2017/10/case-study-gallery-3-1.jpg)

## Confidentiality

We handle your data with **discretion**. It stays with us for 10 days so you can confirm the recovery is complete, and is then permanently and irreversibly deleted from our file servers.

Data is stored on offline servers on an encrypted file system.

## Recovery time

The time needed depends on many factors, such as the type and extent of the damage, the availability of donor parts and how urgent the recovery is.

For urgent cases, one of our engineers is always available.
