RAID is designed to keep services and data running, but hardware or software failures can still lead to data loss.

## RAID and server recovery

Datamind recovers data from all server and drive makes and models, and from the following causes of RAID data loss:

- Damaged or lost RAID configurations
- Failed software or operating system upgrades
- Corrupt or damaged partition tables
- Drives that won't boot, missing or deleted files and folders
- Inaccessible drives
- Clicking drives

We understand how critical these failures are. Our trained engineers recover lost data from failed RAID arrays and servers and, in most cases, can have you back in business within 24–48 hours.

RAID arrays can usually survive a single drive failure without affecting the server. If more than one drive fails, the server may go offline, and **attempts to recover the data yourself can cause permanent data loss**.

Our first step is always to image every working drive. We then rebuild the array on our own workstations, without needing the original controller, card or NAS, so you only need to send us the drives. If that isn't possible, an engineer can come on site.
