Datamind was founded in 2010 by a team of experienced computer engineers from the University of Patras. The company is dedicated to research and to the provision of data recovery services from digital storage media, using the most technologically advanced methods available.

Initially, the company's activity focused on data recovery from hard disks. Through further investment in equipment and training, it subsequently expanded to USB flash drives and SD cards and, from 2014, as SSD technology matured, to solid-state drives.

Datamind invests continuously in modern equipment and in the training of its staff, in order to follow international best practice. Since 2016 the company has been headquartered at the Patras Science Park, a centre for research and innovation, and conducts research in collaboration with the University of Patras.

## History

- **2010 – Foundation.** Datamind is founded by computer engineers from the University of Patras.
- **2013 – USB drives and SD cards.** Investment in equipment for data recovery from USB flash drives and SD cards.
- **2014 – Solid-state drives.** Services are extended to SSDs.
- **2015 – Expansion.** Partnerships throughout Greece and further investment in equipment.
- **2016 – Patras Science Park.** The headquarters relocate to a centre for research and innovation.
- **2017 – Research in monoliths and SSDs.** Infrastructure for recovery from monolithic devices (SD, microSD, Memory Stick, MMC) and research with the University of Patras on scratched disks and SSDs.
- **2018 – Research partnerships.** Collaboration with the data recovery companies LC Technology International (United Kingdom) and IT-DOCTOR, and with the Magnetoelastic Sensors Laboratory of the Department of Chemical Engineering, University of Patras.
- **2020 – M.2 drives.** Data recovery from M.2 and NVMe drives.
