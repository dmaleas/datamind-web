Datamind was founded in 2010 by a group of experienced computer engineers from the University of Patras. The company is dedicated to research and to data recovery from digital media using the most advanced methods available.

Initially, the company focused on recovering data from conventional hard disks. With new investment in equipment and training, it expanded to NAND devices such as USB flash disks and SD cards, and from 2014, as SSD technology matured, to SSD disks.

We continually invest in new technology and in training our people, to keep up with the best international data recovery practices. Since 2016 the company has been based at the Patras Science Park, a research and innovation hub, and conducts data recovery research together with the University of Patras.
