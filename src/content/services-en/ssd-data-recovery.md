## SSD recovery

The reliability of solid-state drives has improved considerably in recent years. Nevertheless, they remain susceptible to failures that can lead to data loss, and their recovery requires specialist expertise and state-of-the-art equipment.

Datamind has completed hundreds of SSD recovery cases and has recovered data from the majority of models on the market.

## Most common causes of failure

- Failure of electronic components
- Controller failure
- Wear of the memory chips due to a high number of write cycles
- Power surges
- Data corruption following a firmware update
