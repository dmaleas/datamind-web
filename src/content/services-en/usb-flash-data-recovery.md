## USB flash drive recovery

Data recovery from USB flash drives presents a considerable challenge, owing to the large number of manufacturers, the absence of common standards and the short period for which each model remains on the market.

The principal advantages of these devices are their portability and durability, as they contain no heads or motors. For this reason they quickly became the preferred medium for transferring data. They are, however, frequently affected by faults, mainly due to their sensitivity to voltage fluctuations.

## Types of failure

- **Electronic failures:** power surges, controller failure
- **Logical failures:** lost partitions, deleted data, file corruption caused by viruses
