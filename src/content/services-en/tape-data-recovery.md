## Tape recovery

Recovering data from tapes is a demanding task. Specific procedures must be followed to recover the information in full, and in certain cases the tape must be disassembled and cleaned in a clean room environment.

Datamind possesses the necessary expertise as well as tape drives from numerous manufacturers and formats, allowing us to read and preserve your valuable archives.

## Supported formats

- Linear Tape Open (LTO) Ultrium
- Digital Linear Tape (DLT and SDLT)
- IBM cartridges and reels (3590 and others)
- Quarter-Inch Cartridge (QIC)
- AIT and S-AIT
- Data8
- Digital Data Storage (DDS)
- Digital Audio Tape (DAT)
