## Ransomware recovery

The encryption of data and the demand of a ransom for the decryption key is a real and growing threat, with attacks intensifying year after year.

Datamind has assisted numerous businesses and individuals in restoring their data, and possesses the expertise and experience required for these cases. Each case differs in duration, cost and approach. Please contact us so that we may assess your situation.

## Immediate actions in the event of a ransomware attack

- **Shut down all computers immediately**, using the normal shutdown procedure.
- **Do not** switch them off at the power button, as this may cause additional damage.
- **Disconnect the local network immediately.**
- Contact us for an immediate assessment of the situation.
- Do not communicate directly with the attackers. There is no guarantee that paying the ransom will result in receiving the decryption key.
